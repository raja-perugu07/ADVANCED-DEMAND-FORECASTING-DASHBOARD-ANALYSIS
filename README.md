# Retail Demand Forecasting & Sales Performance Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail demand forecasting, sales performance, inventory efficiency, promotional impact, and regional sales distribution. The dashboard enables business stakeholders to identify demand gaps, evaluate operational performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Retail organizations often struggle to balance forecasted demand with actual sales performance. Unmet demand can result in lost revenue opportunities, while ineffective promotional strategies can reduce profitability.

This dashboard helps decision-makers:

* Monitor demand fulfillment performance
* Identify demand gaps across product categories
* Measure promotion effectiveness
* Analyze regional sales patterns
* Support inventory planning and resource allocation

## Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle Demand Forecasting Dataset

The dataset contains retail transaction data including:

* Demand
* Units Sold
* Inventory Levels
* Product Categories
* Regions
* Pricing Information
* Discounts and Promotions
* Date-based Sales Records

## Key Findings

### 1. Demand Fulfillment Gap

* Total Demand: 7.93M units
* Total Units Sold: 6.75M units
* Demand Gap: -1.18M units
* Fill Rate: 85%

The business is not fully satisfying customer demand, indicating potential inventory or supply chain constraints.

### 2. Promotion Dependence

Approximately 38% of total sales are driven by promotional activity.

This highlights the importance of promotions in generating revenue while also suggesting potential dependence on discount-based sales strategies.

### 3. Category Performance

Groceries generate the highest demand and sales volume across all categories.

High-demand categories continue to experience unmet demand, creating opportunities for improved inventory planning.

### 4. Pricing Insights

Higher-priced categories do not necessarily generate higher demand.

Customer purchasing behavior appears to be influenced by multiple factors beyond price alone.

### 5. Regional Demand Patterns

The North region contributes the largest share of sales across multiple categories.

Regional demand is unevenly distributed, creating opportunities for targeted inventory and marketing strategies.

## Business Recommendations

* Improve inventory planning for high-demand categories.
* Reduce demand gaps through supply chain optimization.
* Focus promotional spending on high-performing categories.
* Optimize pricing strategies based on category behavior.
* Strengthen distribution efforts in high-demand regions.
